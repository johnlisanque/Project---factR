About Project FACTR

Welcome to Project FACTR

Project FACTR is a web-based mathematics application conceptualized by Mr. Jethro Lago, a mathematics teacher at Leandro Locsin Integrated School (LLIS), with the help and contributions of his students in developing the platform.

FACTR provides randomized calculation problems, giving students different questions for practice instead of always receiving the same problems. It also provides immediate feedback and opportunities for repeated practice, helping students improve their accuracy, understanding, and confidence when solving mathematical problems.

The main goal of FACTR is to strengthen students' numeracy, problem-solving, and independent learning skills while encouraging them to work through problems on their own.

Research Support

Lu (2023) explored the use of interactive web-based tools for teaching and learning. The study explained that these tools can encourage students to actively participate by allowing them to interact with activities, use different inputs, and receive immediate results. It also highlighted how randomized activities and repeated practice can make learning activities more efficient and interactive.

Hosseini & Ferguson (2022) examined an online assignment system that provides students with unique problems using randomly generated values. Their study found improvement in knowledge-based skills among students who used the online system compared with those who completed paper-based assignments. In addition, 88% of surveyed students reported that the online system improved their learning experience. However, the study found no significant improvement in problem-solving skills.

Together, these studies provide a research basis for Project FACTR's use of randomized questions, interactive activities, and repeated practice. They support the use of online learning tools as a way to provide students with varied activities and opportunities to actively participate in their learning and develop their mathematical skills.
